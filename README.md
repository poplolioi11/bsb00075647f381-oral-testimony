# 瘋人誣婦（《點石齋畫報》御十第79圖，1895 年，巴伐利亞邦立圖書館） / Feng ren wu fu [An Accusation, Family Testimony and Official Inquiry]: Transcription Dataset

《點石齋畫報》御十〈瘋人誣婦〉記一名自稱遭妻毆傷的明經前往官署。記事先後載其妻所述既往異常、官員覆問時對他的觀察，以及姪子到署陳述病情並領回。新聞報導呈現家屬證言與官署判斷如何影響處置。館方已有影像及公開機器 OCR。此次按圖重讀補足 OCR 的篇章定位與讀序，並分記原欄、圖字、印款與疑讀。

The Dianshizhai Pictorial recounts the case of a scholar who went to a magistrate’s office and accused his wife of injuring him. It records her account of his prior difficulties, the magistrate’s observations during questioning and the nephew’s testimony and return home with his uncle. The holding library already supplies its scan and machine OCR. This edition records complete article boundaries and source reading order, plus the column divisions, labels, marks, and uncertain readings.

## 方法與覆核 / Methods and review

由 AI 依固定館方影像逐欄讀字，保留原字、版面與疑讀。原紀年依原刊印「御十」。CiNii 書目原御集範圍為 1895.1–1896.1。同一連續出版序號的四百零六號售報告白見於 1895-04-10《申報》，據以定年，廣告日不直接填成原刊日期。　圖說正文、圖字、印與版記分列。人類全文雙校、專家審定、同儕審查及準確率量測尚未進行。實際模型識別與使用量未知。此資料公開供比對與引用，處理程式及影像另存。

An AI assistant transcribed the fixed source images column by column, preserving source forms, layout and uncertain readings. Text, caption, illustration labels, artist marks, seals, and page designations are described separately. Human proofreading, expert review, peer review and accuracy measurement are pending. The actual model identifiers and usage are unknown.
